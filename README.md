# The Belt Line Notch

**An interactive visualization of how incentives and changing conditions can influence AI model behaviour.**


## Overview

The Belt Line Notch is an interactive AI safety visualization that uses an ordinary belt as a metaphor for how AI models adjust their behaviour under changing conditions.

Think about wearing a belt. The notch that feels comfortable when you are hungry may not be the same notch you choose after a meal. The belt has not changed, and the notches were already there. What changes is the condition, and you adjust the buckle accordingly.

AI models also operate under changing conditions. Their responses can be influenced by incentives, objectives, instructions, social pressures, and other contextual factors.

The central question is:

**When a model adjusts its behaviour to changing conditions, does that adjustment preserve its intended objectives—or does it compromise honesty, reliability, or alignment?**

The Belt Line Notch makes this question visible through an interactive metaphor.

## The metaphor

The visualization maps the components of a physical belt to AI behaviour:

| Belt component | AI safety interpretation |
|---|---|
| The belt | The AI model |
| The existing notches | Available behavioural positions or response patterns |
| Changing bodily conditions | Changing context, incentives, objectives, or external pressures |
| The buckle and prong | The model's response adjustment |
| The fit | How well the resulting behaviour remains appropriate to the intended objective |

The important distinction is that **adjustment is not automatically a failure**.

A model should be able to adapt to different users, tasks, and circumstances. The safety concern arises when that adaptation leads to dishonesty, strategic distortion, concealment, or behaviour that departs from the model's intended objectives.



Any future empirical claims will need to be supported by documented experiments and transparent evaluation methods.
